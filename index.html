Where kids dig, build, splash and pretend

Cantona Ranch is built around real, hands-on play: open-ended, a little messy and a long way from a screen. Take a look around.

Sandbox

Dig, scoop, pour and build. Roads, castles, construction sites: whatever they dream up.

Also inside: an Art Studio for painting and crafts, a Science & Discovery area for "what happens if?" questions, and a quiet Story Time corner for a break between adventures.

Why we opened the gate

We invite families to step away from the fast pace of life and reconnect with one another, with nature, and with God.

Forty acres, animals, open space and room to breathe. Children explore freely while the grown-ups finally get to slow down. These are the values behind every visit.

Faith
We believe in the beauty and purpose of God's creation and reflect it in every experience.
Family
Time spent together, where young and old each find their place.
Simplicity
We find the extraordinary in the simple, the real and the authentic.
Connection
With nature, with one another, and with what truly matters.
Peace & rest
A space where adults can breathe and leave the busyness behind.
Joy
The freedom of children to play, explore and be themselves.
Love & care

Driving directions

From The Woodlands15–20 min
  1. Take Woodlands Parkway west.
  2. Turn left onto FM 2978 (Hufsmith–Kuykendahl Rd).
  3. Continue south for about 6–8 miles.
  4. Cantona Homestead is on your right.
From Downtown Tomball10–15 min
  1. Take Main Street / FM 2920 east.
  2. Turn left onto FM 2978 north.
  3. Continue about 3–5 miles.
  4. Cantona Homestead is on your left.
From Spring20–25 min
  1. Take Grand Parkway (TX-99) west toward Tomball.
  2. Exit at FM 2920 and turn right, heading west.
  3. Turn right onto FM 2978 north.
  4. Continue about 3–5 miles. We are on your left.
From Conroe30–35 min
  1. Take I-45 south toward The Woodlands.
  2. Exit onto Woodlands Parkway west.
  3. Turn left onto FM 2978 south.
  4. Continue about 6–8 miles. We are on your right.
From Montgomery35–45 min
  1. Take TX-105 east toward Conroe.
  2. Merge onto I-45 south.
  3. Exit onto Woodlands Parkway west.
  4. Turn left onto FM 2978 south and continue to 24706.

On event days, follow the posted signs and parking attendants.
