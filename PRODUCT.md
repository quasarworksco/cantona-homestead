# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS in a single `index.html` (images in `assets/`), published by GitHub Pages. Fixed by the repository rules in CLAUDE.md.

## Users
Parents in Tomball, The Woodlands, Spring, Magnolia and North Houston, mostly on a phone, deciding where to hold a child's birthday or what to do this weekend. Secondary: teachers and homeschool groups planning field trips, organizations looking for a fundraiser (Spirit Day), and local makers applying to the farmers market. English first, Spanish available.

## Product Purpose
Cantona Homestead is a 40-acre working Texas farm at 24706 FM 2978, Tomball, TX 77375 offering a petting zoo, indoor and outdoor play (Cantona Ranch), birthday parties, field trips, Spirit Days and a free farmers market. This site is a redesign proposal by DGP Global Group for cantonahomestead.com. Success: a visitor books a birthday or buys weekend tickets, or contacts the farm, within one visit.

## Positioning
A real farm with animals plus air-conditioned indoor play rooms, minutes from The Woodlands; faith- and family-centered; a Mesoamerican heritage museum is proposed (not confirmed).

## Operating Context
Booking, ticketing, field-trip / Spirit Day requests, vendor applications and the private portal live in the existing application at cantonahomestead.com. This site does not replace them: every transactional button links there.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Weekend hours: Sat 10 AM-5 PM, Sun 10 AM-3 PM. Tickets: Adult (18+) $10, Children and Youth (1-18) $18, Baby $0.
- Birthdays: Room B & C $450 / 15 children (max 20); Cantona Courtyard $600 / 20 (max 25); Room A $750 / 25 (max 30). 120 minutes + 20 to decorate. Additional child $30. Souvenirs $5 per child. Bounce house with slide $150/hour.
- Field trips: Mon-Fri 9 AM-2 PM, minimum 35 students at $25; adults and siblings $10.
- Farmers market: free admission, 9 AM-1 PM, vendor booth $50, next market Saturday December 5, 2026.
- Heritage Museum is a proposal in development; never present partnerships or the building as confirmed.
- Weather closures are announced by phone; the static site cannot show live notices.
- No sensitive personal data in forms. No emojis; SVG icons only.

## Evidence on Hand
Real farm photographs and copy extracted from cantonahomestead.com (in `assets/`). No testimonials, ratings or visitor counts exist: do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
1. The two money actions (book a birthday, buy tickets) are always one tap away.
2. Facts a parent needs (price, hours, capacity, address) are stated plainly, never hidden behind a click.
3. Real photos of the real farm carry the page.
4. Never claim what the farm has not confirmed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, keyboard operable, reduced-motion respected, bilingual EN/ES.
